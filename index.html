Welcome to SearchBook

Instructions:

This app allows you to search for books based on the category you enter. Enter a category, such as "fantasy" or "history," to view a list of related books.

You can also click on "Show description" to discover more details about each book.

Additionally, you can use the arrow in the bottom right to quickly return to the top of the page or click on SearchBook in the navigation bar to refresh the page.
